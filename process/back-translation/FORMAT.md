# The claim format (both readers use it exactly)

For each chapter:

    # Chapter N

    **The chapter as a whole:** two or three plain sentences: what the chapter says, as an argument or a picture.

    ### Unit 1
    > (the unit's text, quoted exactly)
    - **Says:** a plain, literal paraphrase of the proposition: what is claimed, of what, under what condition
    - **Act:** asserts | advises/commands | asks (real question) | asks (rhetorical: implies ___) | if/when … then | names/defines | describes a person
    - **Who acts / of whom:** the doer and the done-to, as the text gives them; "unstated" if it does not, then who it most likely is
    - **Force:** always/universal · habitual · can/possible · must/necessary · will/future · past · wish
    - **Link:** how this unit connects to the one before: because · therefore/so · but · and · if-then · none
    - **Open:** where the text genuinely supports more than one reading, give each, briefly. "none" if it reads one way only

A unit is one sentence: in English, up to a full stop, colon or semicolon that closes a clause; in Chinese, up to 。 or ； (keep a 、-list or a short parallel pair together if it is plainly one claim).

Keep each entry tight. No evaluation, no suggestions, no comparison with anything outside your packet.
