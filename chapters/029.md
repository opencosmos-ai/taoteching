# Chapter 29

## Translation

Those who would take the world and handle it,  
I see they will not succeed.  
The world is a sacred vessel. It cannot be handled. It cannot be grasped.  
Those who handle it ruin it. Those who grasp it lose it.

So among things, some go ahead, some follow;  
some sigh, some blow;  
some are strong, some are frail;  
some are carried, some are toppled.

Therefore the sage lets go of excess, lets go of extravagance, lets go of grandeur.

## Notes

**為 (*wéi*) → "handle," three times, and the last formula pair closes.** *(2026-09-07, Pass D. Was *force* in all three: 而為之, 不可為也, 為者敗之.)* `glossary/wei-為.md` locks 為 to **do / handle / serve as** — the most frequent word in the glossary, 113 lines across 48 chapters — and **no check could see the breach**, because 為's forbidden list holds only *execute* and *function as*. **為者敗之，執者失之 is verbatim at ch 29 and ch 64**, which already read *"Those who handle it ruin it. Those who grasp it lose it."* The two now match, closing `WORKLIST.md` T2-15.

**What *force* cost was the chapter's argument.** 王弼: 萬物以自然為性，故**可因而不可為**也；可通而不可執也 — *"the countless things take 自然 (self-so) as their nature, therefore they **can be gone along with but not handled**; they can be passed through but not grasped."* **因 (*yīn* — to go along with) is permitted; 為 is not.** *Force* turns that into a warning against violence and lets a gentle manager pass, which the Chinese does not. 河上公 agrees from his side: 欲以**有為**治民, *"wanting to govern the people by having-something-to-do."* **And *force* was already spoken for** — it renders 力 (*lì*) at ch 33 and ch 68, a character covered by `glossary/qiang-強.md`, and **強 stands in this chapter** at 或強或羸.

**取 (*qǔ* — to take) restored.** 將欲**取**天下而為之 had read *"Those who wish to force their will upon the world"*, which dropped the taking and doubled the forcing. The em-dash went with the rewrite, not by decision on `WORKLIST.md` T4-3.

**物 (*wù* — things) restored to block 2.** 故**物**或行或隨 opens with its subject; the English began *"Therefore, sometimes leading."* 王弼 reads the whole series through it: 凡此諸或，言**物事**逆順反覆 — *"all these 'sometimes' speak of things and affairs going against and along, reversing and returning."* That gloss is also what settles the fourth pair, below.

**或▢或▢ four times, four matching lines.** `--formulas 29` shows the frame is the block's entire structure, and the English had varied its shape.

**羸 (*léi*) → "frail."** 說文: **瘦也。从羊** — *"thin; from 羊 (*yáng* — a sheep."* A gaunt animal, not an abstraction. Pairs with 強, locked to *strong*.

**去 (*qù*) → "lets go of," three times.** 說文: **人相違也** — *"people parting from one another."* The manuscript already reads 去 as *let go of* at ch 12, 38 and 72 (去彼取此); ch 29 had one *removes* for three 去.

**甚 · 奢 · 泰 take three distinct words, because 說文 makes them three distinct things.** 甚 is 尤**安樂**也, on 甘 (*gān* — sweet) — **excessive comfort**, not abstract extremity. 奢 is **張也**, on 大 — *stretching wide*, **display**. 泰 is 滑也 — smooth, and by the Warring States **grandeur**. *"The extreme, the extravagant, the excessive"* gave three near-synonyms where the Chinese climbs a scale.

**Two forks recorded, and one is followed against our base — see `notes/manuscript.md` · Ch 29.** 或挫或隳 reads 或**載**或隳 in 河上公, and we follow him: 挫 is 說文 摧也 (*to break*) beside 隳 (*to ruin*), which gives **two wrecking words where every other pair in the series is an opposition**. 載 is 乘也。从**車** — *to carry*, on the cart radical — so *carried* against *toppled* restores it, and 王弼's own 逆順反覆 gloss demands one. **Both witnesses here are transmitted, not excavated** — Guodian does not carry this chapter — so this is an editorial call on internal grounds and is logged as such. 或歔或吹 keeps the base: 河上公 reads 呴 and glosses the pair 呴溫也，吹寒也 (*warm against cold*), where our 歔 is 說文 欷也…一曰出气也, *to sob; to let out breath*. **"Some sigh, some blow"** takes the base; the temperature reading is recorded, not adopted. *(The old *"breathing heavily / breathing easily"* also had the effort backwards — 吹 is the forceful one.)*
