# Chapter 26

## Translation

The heavy is the root of the light.  
Stillness is the ruler of the agitated.

Therefore, the noble travels all day without leaving the baggage wagons.  
Even where the sights are glorious, they dwell at ease and stay apart.

How can the lord of ten thousand chariots  
make their own body light in the world?  
Lightness loses the root.  
Agitation loses the ruler.

## Notes

**君子 (*jūn zǐ*) → "the noble," swept with ch 31 on 2026-09-07.** *(Was "the sage.")* **聖人 (*shèng rén*) is the sage** and carried 38 English lines; 君子 is literally *a ruler's child*, the **Confucian** honorific, and giving it 聖人's word made Laozi's figure and Confucius's the same person. Full argument in ch 31's notes. **Note the seam:** 君子 named a male class and *the noble* is deliberately neutral. **君 (*jūn*) now reads *ruler* in both places** *(closed 2026-09-20, T2-6)* — it had been *ruler* at 靜為躁君 and *mastery* at 躁則失君, three lines apart in a chapter whose last two lines are a matched pair.

**The whole chapter reworked, 2026-09-20** *(T2-1 · T2-5 · T2-6)*.

**輕 (*qīng* — light) had three Englishes and the chapter turns on it.** 重 (*zhòng* — heavy) against 輕 is the first line's pair and the last two lines' verdict, and *ungrounded* had replaced *light* at all three, hiding the pair. **輜重 (*zī zhòng* — the heavy baggage train) had become *sustenance***, losing the 重 that line 1 depends on; 王弼 reads it plainly, 以重為本故不離, *"takes the heavy as root, therefore does not leave it."*

**靜 (*jìng*) → *stillness*, closing T2-5.** It read *Silence* here alone against seven lines elsewhere (16 ×2, 45 ×2, 57, 61 ×2).

**奈何萬乘之主，而以身輕天下 had lost three of its parts.** 萬乘 (*wàn shèng* — ten thousand chariots) went to *a great ruler*; 身 became *their being*; and **天下 (*tiān xià* — the world) was deleted outright.** That line is one of only three in the book where 身 and 天下 stand together — with ch 13's 以身為天下 twice and ch 54's ladder — and `chapters/013.md` cites this line as the support for its own closing couplet. 河上公: 王者至尊，而以其身行輕躁乎. See `glossary/shen-身.md`.

**燕處超然 → *they dwell at ease and stay apart*.** *Their equanimity is undisturbed* rendered no character; 燕處 is dwelling at ease and 超然 is standing clear.
