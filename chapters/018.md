# Chapter 18

## Translation

When the great Tao is discarded, humaneness and duty appear.  
When cleverness and erudition arise, great hypocrisy appears.  
When kin are out of harmony, devotion and tenderness appear.  
When nations grow clouded and fall into disorder, loyal ministers appear.

## Notes

**國家昏亂 → *nations grow clouded and fall into disorder*.** *(Shalom's call, 2026-09-21.)* 亂 was *chaos*, a **cosmological** word in English where 亂 is political and domestic; see `glossary/luan-亂.md`. The provisional line of 2026-09-20 is now settled.


**國家昏亂 → *nations grow clouded and fall into chaos*.** *(2026-09-20, `WORKLIST.md` T2-54.)* 昏 (*hūn* — clouded) had been **absorbed into 亂's English** and not rendered at all; the line read *"When nations fall into chaos."* Two characters, one word.

**⚠ This line is provisional.** 亂 (*luàn*) still reads *chaos* here, and `glossary/luan-亂.md` recommends **disorder** against it — 說文 defines 亂 as **治也**, *to bring to order*, and ch 64 prints the pair 治之於未亂. The entry is `status: open` pending 治, so the swap is owed, not taken. `WORKLIST.md` T2-55.


*Revised 2026-08-28 under the 仁 and 慈 locks (`glossary/ren-仁.md`, `glossary/ci-慈.md`).*

**孝慈 had lost half of itself.** The verse read *"filial piety"* for 孝慈, so 慈 (*cí* — tenderness) had no English at all. The two graphs point opposite ways: 孝 is 老 (*lǎo* — old) abbreviated over 子 (*zǐ* — child), **the child bearing up the elder**; 慈 is 心 (*xīn* — heart) under a phonetic carrying *to grow*, the heart increasing toward its offspring. 河上公 makes it a circuit rather than a list: 乃有孝慈**相牧養**也, *"then there is 孝慈, tending each other."* Hence **devotion and tenderness**.

**六親 → "kin," and the number is deliberately not in the English.** 王弼: 六親**父子兄弟夫婦**也 — *"the six kin are parents and children, brothers, husband and wife."* Three **pairs**, counted as six **persons** — so the number enumerates people while the line is about relations, and English cannot carry both without a gloss inside the verse. *Families* was the wrong unit (households, not relations); *the six kin* makes the reader stop for a number that is doing no work in the argument.

**The scare quotes are gone from 忠臣.** The Chinese is straight, and the irony is structural — all four lines have one shape, and the shape is the claim. Quotation marks were the edition's voice telling the reader what to hear.

**大道 → the great Tao**, restoring 大, which ch 34 and ch 53 already carry.

**On what the chapter is doing.** 河上公: 大道之時，家有孝子，戶有忠信，仁義**不見**也 — *"in the time of the great Tao, every family had devoted children and every household loyalty and trust, and humaneness and duty **were not visible**."* Not absent. Unnamed, because nothing had gone wrong that would make anyone name them. See `notes/manuscript.md` · Ch 19 for the Guodian fork, and for the Yongle Dadian's joining of this chapter to the next.
