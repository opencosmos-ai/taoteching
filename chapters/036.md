# Chapter 36

## Translation

If you would draw it in, you must first stretch it out.  
If you would weaken it, you must first strengthen it.  
If you would bring it down, you must first raise it up.  
If you would take from it, you must first give to it.

This is called the hidden clarity.  
The soft and yielding overcome the hard and strong.

A fish cannot leave deep water.  
A state's tools of advantage cannot be shown.

## Notes

**國之利器不可以示人 → "A state's tools of advantage cannot be shown."** *(Shalom's call, 2026-10-14. Was "must never be shown.")* The two clauses are one parallel, 魚不可脫於淵 and 國之利器不可以示人, and the English had split them into *cannot* and *must never*. **王弼 (*Wáng Bì*) reads both as one consequence:** 魚脫於淵則必見失矣。利國器而立刑以示人，亦必失也, *"a fish that leaves the deep is surely lost; to show the state's tools to people is also surely lost."* 亦 (*yì* — also) joins them and 必 (*bì* — surely) makes both predictions. A fish is not forbidden to leave the water. It dies. **The cost, named:** 河上公 (*Héshàng Gōng*) and 韓非 (*Hán Fēi*) hear advice to a ruler (不可以示執事之臣, *"must not be shown to the ministers"*), and *must never* was honest to that reading. The chapter's other *musts* stay, because 必 prints them. Full argument: `glossary/ke-可.md`.

**Rewritten 2026-09-05** (Pass D, `WORKLIST.md` D36 · T3-4). Full reasoning in `notes/translation.md`; three forks in `notes/manuscript.md`.

- **The four clauses are conditional instructions, not abstract laws.** 將欲X之，必固Y之 ×4 — the frame repeats and the English must. 將欲 and both 之 had been deleted, the verbs nominalized, and **固 turned from an adverb into the adjective *"firm."*** 河上公 glosses 固 with 先 (*xiān* — first) all four times, and 韓非's worked examples are sequences in time.
- 利器 → **tools of advantage**, not *sharp instruments*. All three commentators read an instrument of governing: 韓非 賞罰 (reward and punishment) · 王弼 利國之器 · 河上公 權道. *advantage* is 利's covered lock in `wu-you-無有.md`; *edge* was already 銳's at ch 9.
- 淵 → **deep water**, not *the deep abyss*. 說文 回水也…左右，岸也 — circling water, banks left and right. **Closes T2-24's ch 36 half.**
- 示人 → **shown**. 說文 defines 示 with the phrase 所以示人也, so the audience is inside the verb; *to others* restated it.
- **Open, not settled here:** 微 (*wēi*) reads *hidden* here against **faint** at ch 15 and 64 · T2-34. And 利器 as a compound also stands at ch 57 · T2-33.

**微 (*wēi*) — the thread from ch 14.** *(Shalom's call, 2026-09-10 — `WORKLIST.md` T2-52.)* 是謂微明 renders **微** as *hidden*. **Chapter 14 is where the book names this character**: 搏之不得，名曰微, given there as *"its name is the intangible."* 說文解字 has 微 as 隱行也, *"to move concealed,"* on 彳 (*chì* — the step radical) — which is this chapter's word almost exactly, since 微明 is clarity that does not announce itself. Ch 14 renders the meaning rather than the word for that chapter's legibility, so the link is carried here. Also ch 15 and ch 64, where 微 reads *faint*.
