# Chapter 39

## Translation

These received the one long ago:  
Sky received the one, and so became clear.  
Earth received the one, and so became settled.  
Spirit received the one, and so became numinous.  
The valley received the one, and so became full.  
The countless things received the one, and so came to life.  
Rulers received the one, and so serve as the world's standard.

Follow it through:  
Sky with no way to be clear is likely to split.  
Earth with no way to be settled is likely to burst.  
Spirit with no way to be numinous is likely to cease.  
The valley with no way to be full is likely to run dry.  
The countless things with no way to live are likely to die out.  
Rulers with no way to be prized and high are likely to topple.

So the prized takes the cheap as its root,  
and the high takes the low as its footing.  
This is why rulers call themselves orphaned, alone, and unworthy.  
Is this not taking the cheap as the root? Is it not?

So reduce a cart to the count of its parts, and there is no cart.  
Do not want to be gleaming like jade,  
or common like stone.

## Notes

**Rewritten 2026-09-05** (Pass D, `WORKLIST.md` D39 · T3-6). Full reasoning in `notes/translation.md`; five forks in `notes/manuscript.md`.

- **得一 means holding what makes you what you are**, not living off the quality it produces. 王弼: 用一以致清耳，非用清以清也 — *"it is by using the one that clarity is brought about, not by using clarity to be clear."* 得 → **received** (Shalom's line): 王弼 calls the one 其母, *its mother*.
- **The mirror was inverted.** It broke at pair five, where the Chinese holds (生/生, read *"come into being"* / *"lacked **presence**"* — 有's locked word, and 有 is absent), and was smoothed at pair six, where the Chinese breaks (為天下**貞** / 無以**貴高**, both *"anchor"*). 貴高 is the hinge into 故貴以賤為本，高以下為基.
- 為天下貞 → **serve as the world's standard**, matching ch 22 and ch 28 ×3 (T2-15, settled 2026-09-01). We had a fourth rendering of a settled formula. 貞 is 說文 卜問也 — what is consulted to know what is right.
- 無以X → **with no way to be X**: the loss is of the *means*, not the quality.
- 貴/賤 → **prized / cheap**, not *noble / humble*. Both are 貝 (cowry) graphs: 說文 物不賤也 and 賈少也. **Price words, not moral ones.**
- **故致數譽無譽 → the cart.** We depart from the base: the Siku editors call 譽 a scribal error and both commentaries explain a vehicle. Lands ch 11's image.
- **The close is *neither* jade *nor* stone**, not *"not jade but stone."* One 不欲 governs both clauses, and both commentators say so outright. *Instead* was supplied.
- **Two strains, named:** 琭琭/珞珞 have no 說文 entries; 靈 → *numinous* fails the pointing test. 神 and 靈 want an entry together · T2-37.
