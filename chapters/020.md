# Chapter 20

## Translation

Cut off learning and there is no worry.

Between a respectful yes and a careless yeah,  
how much distance?  
Between good and bad,  
how far apart?  
What people fear must be feared.  
How wild it is, and without end!

The crowd is merry, as if at a great feast,  
as if climbing a terrace in spring.  
I alone am moored, and show no sign,  
like an infant who has not yet learned to smile,  
listless, as if with nowhere to return.

The crowd all have more than enough.  
I alone seem to have missed out.  
I have the heart of a fool!  
How muddled!  
The worldly are bright. I alone am clouded.  
The worldly are sharp. I alone am dull.

Calm, like the sea.  
Blown along, as if never stopping.

The crowd all have something to use themselves for.  
I alone am stubborn, and look coarse.  
I alone differ from others:  
I prize feeding from the mother.

## Notes

**昏昏 (*hūn hūn*) → *clouded*, not *dark*.** *(Shalom's call, 2026-09-20. `WORKLIST.md` T2-54.)* 說文: **日冥也。从日氐省。氐者，下也** — *"the sun gone dark; from 日 (the sun) and an abbreviated 氐, and 氐 means below."* The sun under the line: dusk. *Dark* belongs to 玄 (*xuán*), six verse lines including ch 1's 玄之又玄, and there is no 玄 here. With this line and ch 57, **玄 has its word to itself again** — `--english "dark"` is 6 lines, 6 backed.

**河上公 (*Héshàng Gōng*) glosses this with the same compound he uses for 昧 at ch 41** — 我獨若昏 → **如闇昧也** — so for him 昏 and 昧 name nearly one state. The English keeps them a step apart, *clouded* against *murky*, which is the distance that reading suggests. He reads the other half as 明且達也, *"bright and penetrating,"* and 王弼 as 耀其光也, *"they dazzle with their light."*

**A fork, unlogged until now.** The Siku editors note on 王弼: 〔案昏昏**河上公注本作若昏**〕 — *"for 昏昏, Heshang Gong's commentary text reads 若昏."* Our base doubles the character; his hedges it to *as if 昏*. We keep the base. Recorded in `sources/variants.yaml`.

**Not changed, and worth seeing:** this chapter runs on **reduplication** — 昭昭 · 昏昏 · 察察 · 悶悶 · 沌沌 — and the English renders every one as a single plain word. Whether that doubling should show is unasked.
