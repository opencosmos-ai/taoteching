# Chapter 67

## Translation

The world says my Tao is great, and looks like nothing.  
Only because it is great does it look like nothing.  
If it looked like anything, it would long since have been slight.

I have three treasures. I hold them and keep them safe.  
The first is tenderness.  
The second is frugality.  
The third is never pushing to be first in the world.

Tenderness, and so you can be brave.  
Frugality, and so you can reach far.  
Never pushing to be first in the world, and so you can become chief of the vessels.

Now: to abandon tenderness and grab at courage,  
to abandon frugality and grab at reach,  
to abandon the rear and grab at the front.  
That is death.

Tenderness. Fight with it and you win, guard with it and you stand firm.  
When nature would rescue someone, it shields them with tenderness.

## Notes

**天下皆謂 → "The world says."** *(2026-10-14, Shalom's call. Was: "Everyone says.")* 天下 (*tiān xià*) is *the world*, as in ch 2's 天下皆知 (*"when the world knows"*). *Everyone* is now 百姓 (*bǎi xìng*)'s (`glossary/baixing-百姓.md`), and ch 17's 百姓皆謂 is this line's frame with the people in the subject slot. *The world* says the Tao is great; *everyone* says we did it ourselves. The note below keeps the 2026-09-06 call on 道 (*dào*), which stands.

**天下皆謂我道大 → "Everyone says my Tao is great."** *(2026-09-06, Shalom's call. Was: "Everyone says I am vast, and look like nothing.")* **道 (*dào*) had been dropped and the whole passage handed to the speaker.** The Chinese says 我**道**大 — *my Tao is great* — and the three lines that follow are about the Tao, not about the person speaking. With 道 restored the subject runs *it* throughout, and 王弼's argument lands: 肖則失其所以為大矣 — *"to resemble [things] is to lose what makes it great."* The Tao looks like nothing because anything it resembled would have shrunk it to that thing's size.

**大 → "great"**, completing the sweep begun at ch 25 — 大 is *great* in 41 lines and was *vast* only here and at ch 25.

**細 (*xì*) → "slight," matching ch 63.** *(Was "petty.")* 細 occurs in two chapters and had two Englishes; ch 63's 為大於其細 and 天下大事，必作於細 already read **slight** twice. *Petty* also imported a moral verdict the character does not carry — 細 is the **silk radical** 糸, thread-fine, a matter of size.

**夫唯 (*fú wéi*) reads "Only because" here, and now reads it everywhere.** *(2026-10-19, Shalom's call. This note had left the line untouched, a formula across 11 chapters and 12 lines that carried five Englishes.)* The frame 夫唯…故 / 是以 (*gù / shì yǐ* — therefore) reads *"Only because X, Y,"* with the clause after it inverted, in all nine of its lines. 唯 (*wéi*) is *only*, and 河上公's gloss here doubles it: 唯**獨**名德大者為身害, *"only for one whose name for integrity is great does it become a harm to the body."* `glossary/wei-唯.md` · `WORKLIST.md` T2-43.

**Three of the four holding characters meet on this page, and two of them were sharing an English.** *(2026-09-08, Pass E, `WORKLIST.md` T2-19 — the holding family.)* 我有三**寶**，**持**而**保**之 puts 持 (*chí* — to hold), 保 (*bǎo* — to keep safe) and 寶 (*bǎo* — treasure) in one line; 以**守**則固 adds 守 (*shǒu*); 以慈**衛**之 adds 衛 (*wèi* — to escort, to guard). **守 had read *defend* and 衛 had read *guards*.** Now 守 takes its locked flexion — 以戰則勝，以守則固 → *"Fight with it and you win, **guard** with it and you stand firm"* — which is 說文's own second sense for 守 (寺府之事者, the business of a court, sentry included) and which 河上公 (*Héshàng Gōng*) expands to 以**守衛**則堅固. 衛 moves to *shields*, and 固 (*gù* — 說文 四塞也, *"blocked on all four sides"*) gives up *hold firm* for *stand firm*. See `glossary/shou-守.md`, `glossary/bao-保.md`, `glossary/chi-持.md`.
