# Chapter 81

## Translation

Trustworthy words are not pretty.  
Pretty words are not trustworthy.

Those who are masterful do not argue.  
Those who argue are not masterful.

Those who know have not heard it all.  
Those who have heard it all do not know.

The sage does not hoard.  
Having done for others, they have more.  
Having given to others, they have more still.

The way of nature benefits and does not harm.  
The way of the sage acts and does not contend.

## Notes

**善 → masterful, not "good."** The same overlay as ch 79. 善者不辯 (*shàn zhě bù biàn*) is a claim about skill, not morals: 河上公 glosses 辯者 as 巧言也 (*qiǎo yán yě* — "clever speech") and its cost as 舌致患也 ("the tongue brings on trouble").

**辯 (*biàn*) → argue.** The graph is 辡 — two 辛 (*xīn*, tattooing knives, the mark of the convict) facing each other — with 言 (*yán*, words) set between them: **words between two disputants.** The courtroom is in the character, so "eloquence" is downstream of it, not in it. Ch 45's 大辯若訥 (*dà biàn ruò nè*) keeps "eloquence" for now — there 辯 is praised and paired with 訥 (stammering), a different slot.

**美 (*měi*) → pretty.** Both commentators read ornament, not beauty. 王弼: 本在樸也 — "the root lies in the uncarved" (樸, *pǔ*, our locked term), making *uncarved* the antonym of 美. 河上公: 孳孳華詞 ("busily flowery phrasing") and 飾偽多空虛 ("decorated falsehood, much of it empty").

**博 (*bó*) → *have heard it all*.** 十 (*shí*, ten — the complete set) over 尃 (*fū*, to spread out): **spread wide.** 河上公 names both sides — 博者多見聞 ("the 博 has seen and heard much") against 不博者守一元也 ("the un-博 holds fast to the one origin," 守 our locked term). The chapter is not against knowing things; it is against breadth bought with concentration, and 河上公 says it costs rather than ranks: 不知者失要真也 — 失 (*shī*, to **lose**), 要 (*yào*, essential), 真 (*zhēn*, real).

**lock-ok: borrowed rendering.** Ch 81 contains no 聞 (*wén* — to hear); *heard* is taken from 河上公's 多見聞 rather than from this chapter's own Chinese, so `concordance.py --english "heard"` will show ch 81 unbacked. Deliberate, and argued in `notes/translation.md` · Ch 81.

**不積 is not "owns nothing."** 積 (*jī*) is 禾 (*hé*, standing grain) with 責 (*zé*, to gather in): **grain heaped in a granary.** Ch 59 uses the same character approvingly — 重積德 (*zhòng jī dé*, "a heavy accumulation of integrity") — and 河上公 resolves the two in one line: 聖人積德不積財 ("the sage accumulates integrity, not wealth"). *Hoard* keeps the granary and the disapproval without contradicting ch 59.

**The book's last verb is 為, not 無為.** 為而不爭 (*wéi ér bù zhēng*) — the sage **does**, and is qualified only by *not contending*. See `notes/reading.md`.

**辯 → *argue* now stands in both chapters, and the deferral in the note above is closed.** *(2026-08-31, Pass C, `WORKLIST.md` T2-9.)* That note read: *"Ch 45's 大辯若訥 keeps 'eloquence' for now — there 辯 is praised and paired with 訥 (stammering), a different slot."* **The premise was wrong.** Ch 45's 大X若Y frame does not endorse its X; the line directly above 大辯若訥 is 大巧若拙, and 巧 (*qiǎo*) is condemned at ch 19 (絕巧棄利) and ch 57 (人多伎巧). Ch 45 now reads *"Great argument seems halting."*

**The commentaries put the two chapters together rather than apart.** 王弼 (*Wáng Bì*) on ch 45: 大辯因物而言，己**無所造** — *"great 辯 speaks according to things; the self invents nothing."* 河上公 (*Héshàng Gōng*) on this chapter's 善者不辯: 善者以道修身，**不綵文**也 — *"the masterful cultivate themselves by the Tao and do not ornament their words."* **Both say: do not manufacture speech.**
